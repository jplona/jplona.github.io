Caramel Apple Cake

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Cakes
Preparation time
20 min
Servings
6

Ingredients

Instructions

Preheat oven to 350F. Mix bisquick & sugar in bowl. Stir in milk until blended. Pour into ungreased 9x9x2 pan. Top with sliced apples & sprinkle with lemon juice. Mix brown sugar & cinnamon; sprinkle over apples. Pour boiling water over apples. Bake 50 - 60 min or until done.
