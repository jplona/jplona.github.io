Walnut Balls

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Cookies
Prep time
20 min
Servings
5 doz

Ingredients

Instructions

Cream butter & sugar. Add all ingredients & mix well. Shape into balls 1/2" in diameter. Bake 375 F for 15 min. Roll in powdered sugar while warm, then again after cool.

Yield: approx. 5 doz.
