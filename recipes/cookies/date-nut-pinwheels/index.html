Date-Nut Pinwheels

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Cookies
Prep time
30 min
Servings
5 doz

Ingredients

Instructions

FILLING: Cook 3/4# pitted chopped dates or prunes with 1/3 cup sugar & 1/3 cup water until slightly thick. Stir constantly. Cool. Add 1/2 cup nuts.

Mix crisco, butter, sugar, egg & vanilla. Mix in dry ingredients. Form into a roll. Wrap in waxed paper. Chill until firm. Divide dough in half. Roll each piece in waxed paper to form a rectangle 11" x 7". Spread with filling. Roll from the 11" side & seal edges. Wrap rolls in waxed paper & chill several hours. Cut in 1/4" slices. Place on lightly greased cookie sheet & bake in preheated oven @ 400 F for 10 min.
