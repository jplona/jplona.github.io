Game Hens

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Poultry
Preparation time
30 min
Servings
4

Ingredients

Instructions

Preheat broiler. Cut hens in half, discarding backbones. To make glaze, combine 2 tsp of oil , the marmalade & curry powder. Cook over medium heat about 1 min. Stir in vinegar. Brush hens with remaining 2 tsp oil, salt & pepper. Place hens, skin side down & broil about 5" from heat source for 10 min. Brush with glaze & turn skin side up. Broil 5 min more until brown.
