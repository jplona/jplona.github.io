Cod Fillets

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Seafood
Prep time
15 min.
Servings
6

Ingredients

Instructions

Sprinkle bottom of greased shallow baking dish with bread crumbs. Wipe fish with a damp cloth & place cod in the dish. Mix together the remaining ingredients & pour over the fish. Sprinkle with paprika & baste several times. Bake in hot oven @ 450 F about 10 minutes per each inch.
