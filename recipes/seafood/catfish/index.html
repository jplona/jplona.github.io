Catfish

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Seafood
Prep time
20 min
Servings
6

Ingredients

Instructions

Drop fillets in brown paper bag with Cornmeal. Shake to cover all sides of fish. Heat oil until "SMOKING HOT". Cook on high until catfish floats to top & reaches a golden brown.
