Broiled Catfish

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Seafood
Prep time
15 min
Servings
6

Ingredients

Instructions

Clean, wash & dry fish. Combine remaining ingredients except lemon wedges & mix thoroughly. Place fish on a well-greased broiler pan; brush with sauce. Broil about 3 inches from source of heat for 4 to 6 min. Turn carefully & brush other side with sauce. Broil 4 to 6 min longer, basting occasionally, until fish flakes easily when tested with a fork. Serve with lemon wedges.
