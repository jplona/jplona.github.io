Cheese Ball

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Appetizers
Prep time
15 min.
Servings
20

Ingredients

Instructions

Combine softened cream cheese & cheddar cheese, mixing until well blended. Add remaining ingredients; mix well. Chill. Shape into ball; roll in chopped nuts.
