Mock Whip Cream Icing

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Frosting
Prep time
15 min
Recipe from
Violet Andrews

Ingredients

Instructions

Combine sugar & flour; add milk & egg. Cook over low heat stirring constantly until thick like custard. Remove from heat & cool stirring occasionally. Cream shortening, conf. sugar & vanilla. Add cold custard mixture & beat for 10 min or more until creamy. Frosts 1 large cake.
