Creamy Frosting

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Frosting
Prep time
20 min

Ingredients

Instructions

Mix & set aside pudding & milk. Cream margarine, shortening, powdered sugar & vanilla. Add pudding . Beat for 8 to 10 min until creamy. Yield: frosting for one 9" cake.
