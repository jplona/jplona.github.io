Parsnip Fry Cakes

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Vegetables
Prep time
10 min
Servings
4

Ingredients

Instructions

Mash cooked parsnips. Combine parsnips with next five ingredients. Mix well. Shape into patties 1/2" thick. Coat both sides with flour. Fry in bacon drippings over medium heat, turning to brown. Serve immediately.
