Eggplant Parmesan

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Vegetables
Prep time
20 min
Servings
4

Ingredients

Instructions

Peel eggplant. Cut into thin slices. Salt & pepper. Flour & brown on both sides in small amount of oil. Drain on paper. Layer eggplant & tomato in a greased casserole. Sprinkle each layer with chopped onion, bits of butter, brown sugar. Place layer of tomatoes on top. Sprinkle top with buttered crumbs. Layer finally with slices of cheese. Bake 375F 45 min. Cover to prevent browning of cheese.
