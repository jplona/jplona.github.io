Lemon Squares

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Desserts
Preparation time
20 min
Servings
14

Ingredients

Filling

Crust

Layer Ingredients

Instructions

Cook filling as directed. Mix crust ingredients and press into 10 x 15 cookie sheet. Bake 15 to 20 min at 350 F. Cool crust completely. Beat 12 oz cream cheese, 1 1/2 cups conf. sugar until smooth. Spread on cooled crust, the cooler the better. Add cool pie filling on top. Add cool whip.
