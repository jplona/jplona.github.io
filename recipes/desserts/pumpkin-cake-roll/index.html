Pumpkin Cake Roll

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Desserts
Prep time
30 min
Servings
10

Ingredients

Filling

Instructions

Preheat oven to 350F. Combine sugar, eggs, pumpkin, flour, baking soda, salt & Cinnamon. Bake 15 min in a greased & floured 10x15" jellyroll pan lined with waxed paper. Remove & turn out on towel sprinkled with confectioners' sugar. Roll up lengthwise in towel. Leave in towel for 5 to 10 min. Unroll & cool. Beat all filling ingredients & spread on open cake. Set cake on towel & roll up. Refrigerate.
