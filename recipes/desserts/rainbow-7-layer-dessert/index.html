Rainbow 7 Layer Dessert

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Desserts
Prep time
2 hrs
Servings
12

Ingredients

Instructions

Dissolve 1st pkg in 1 cup hot water. Add 1/2 c cold water. Pour into 9x13 glass dish & refrig. until firm, about 30 min. Meanwhile, bring milk to a boil, add sugar until dissolved. Remove from heat. Soften unflavored gel in 1/2 c cold water. Mix well & add to hot milk mixture. Add sour cream & vanilla. Beat until smooth. Pour 1/3 over 1 1/2 c of firm Jello in pan. Refrig. until cream layer is firm. Keep remaining cream mixture at room temp. Repeat with remaining Jellos.
