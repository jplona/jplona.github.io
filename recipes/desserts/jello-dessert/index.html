Jello Dessert

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Desserts
Preparation time
30 min
Servings
12

Ingredients

Instructions

Prepare Jello. Let partially set. Beat egg yolks with sugar, add melted butter; mix. Add pineapple & nuts. Beat egg whites until stiff & blend in. Line a 9 x 13" dish with graham crackers. Pour 1/2 of mixture over crackers. Add another layer of crackers & pour remaining mixture. Add layer of crackers; pour partially set jello. Refrigerate until jello is completely set. Top with whipping cream.
