Zucchini & Sausage Quiche

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Quiche
Prep time
20 min
Servings
6

Ingredients

Instructions

Preheat oven to 450F Bake the pie shell for 8 min Saute zucchini in butter until tender. Drain. Spread cooked zucchini on bottom of pastry shell, sprinkle with crumbled cooked sausage & then sprinkle Swiss cheese over the top. Combine eggs, milk, cream, Parmesan cheese & seasonings, mixing well. Pour into shell & bake at 450F for 15 min. Lower temp to 350F & bake until center is firm but still soft, about 20 to 30 min.
