Ham & Cheese Quiche

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Quiche
Preparation time
30 min
Servings
6

Ingredients

Instructions

In greased deep 9" pie dish layer ham, bacon, cheeses, onion & mushrooms. Set aside. In medium bowl, combine eggs, milk, biscuit mix, salt & pepper. Beat well. Pour over ham & cheese mixture. Bake in preheated oven 10 min @ 400F. Reduce temp. to 350F & bake 40 - 45 min.
