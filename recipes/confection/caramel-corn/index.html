Caramel Corn

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Confection
Preparation time
1 1/2 hrs
Servings
8

Ingredients

Instructions

Blend over heat, butter, brown sugar, & Karo syrup. Stir constantly until syrup comes to a boil. Boil on simmer 5 min without stirring. Take off heat, add vanilla, baking soda & salt. Pour over 2 batches popped popcorn. Bake in oven uncovered for 1 hr at 250F. Mix every 15 min.
