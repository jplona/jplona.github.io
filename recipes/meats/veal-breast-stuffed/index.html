Veal Breast - Stuffed

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Meats
Prep time
20 min
Servings
4 - 6

Ingredients

Instructions

Trim Breast of Veal. Slice a big pocket along top portion of rib bones. Saute 1/2# chicken livers in 2 tbsp butter. Chop finely. Set aside. Saute onion & celery in 2 tbsp butter until soft. Combine livers, vegetables, bread stuffing, parsley, salt & pepper. Add milk. Add eggs. Stuff veal to thickness of 1" across breast. Skewer breast with 2 wooden skewers. Roast 2 to 2 1/2 hrs @ 350F.
