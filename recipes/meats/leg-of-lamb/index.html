Leg of Lamb

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Meats
Prep time
15 min
Servings
6

Ingredients

Instructions

Slit outer layer of fat & insert pieces of garlic under fat. Rub salt & pepper on exterior of leg of lamb. Place on rack in roasting pan. Sprinkle with basil & lemons. Roast, lightly covered in 350F oven for 1 hour. Remove cover & roast 1 hour longer. Serve with mint jelly.
