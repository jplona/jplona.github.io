Split Pea Soup

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Soups
Preparation time
20 min
Servings
6

Ingredients

Instructions

In deep pot combine peas, water, ham shank, onion, chicken bouillon & seasonings. Simmer uncovered 1 1/2 hrs. Remove ham shank, trim meat off bone & return to pot. Stir in carrots & celery. Simmer uncovered 1 1/2 to 2 hrs or until soup reaches desired thickness.
