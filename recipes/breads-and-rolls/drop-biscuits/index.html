Drop Biscuits

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Breads & Rolls
Preparation time
30 min
Servings
2 doz

Ingredients

Instructions

Work with hands to oatmeal consistency. Whisk all ingredients. Brush with egg wash. ( 1 beaten egg with 1 Tbsp milk ) Bake 425 F for 10-12 min.

Suggestion: Divide dough in half. Add chives to make 1 dozen.
