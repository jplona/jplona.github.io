Banana Bread

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Breads & Rolls
Prep time
30 min
Servings
10-12

Ingredients

Instructions

Cream margarine, Crisco, & sugar. Add eggs & broken pcs. banana. Add flour, salt, & baking soda. Stir in Walnuts. Pour into greased & floured 4" x 12" loaf pan. Bake @ 350F 50 to 60 min.

Yield: 1 loaf
