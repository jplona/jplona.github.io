Cranberry Salad

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Salads
Preparation time
15 min
Servings
10

Ingredients

Instructions

In a large bowl soften gelatine in cold water. Heat 1 1/2 cups juice, add sugar & salt to gelatin & stir until dissolved. Add lemon juice & remaining 2 1/2 cups cranberry juice. Pour 3/4 cups in 1 1/2 qt mold. Chill until firm. Chill remaining gelatine until it begins to set, then fold in apple, celery & walnuts. Put in mold & chill overnight. Serve with dressing.

Family Note

Dressing: equal parts of mayo and sour cream.
