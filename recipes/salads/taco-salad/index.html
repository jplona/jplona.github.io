Taco Salad

From the kitchen of: Mary C. Plona

Category
Salads
Preparation time
30 min
Servings
8

Ingredients

Instructions

Brown meat & onion. Drain off excess fat. Add remaining ingredients. Toss with Catalina dressing.

Optional - Add one bottle of Mild Salsa or Mild Sauce (Ortega) to browned meat if desired. Use more meat if desired.
