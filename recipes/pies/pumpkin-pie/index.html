Pumpkin Pie

From the kitchen of Mary C. Plona

Category
Pies
Prep time
30 min
Servings
8

Ingredients

Instructions

Preheat oven to 425 F. In large mixer bowl, combine all ingredients except pastry shell; mix well. Pour into pastry shell. Bake 15 min. Reduce oven temperature to 350 F; bake 35 to 40 minutes longer or until knife inserted 1 inch from edge comes out clean. Cool. Garnish as desired.
