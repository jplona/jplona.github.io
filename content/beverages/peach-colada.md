## Ingredients
- 2 Lg, very ripe Peaches, less pits
- 1/4 cup Mandarin Orange, drained
- 2 cups Ice
- 1/4 cup Unsweetened Coconut Milk
- 2 tbsp Orange Juice concentrate
- Fresh Peaches for Garnish

## Instructions
Blend all ingredients in blender until smooth. Serve in tall glasses & garnish with peach if desired.
