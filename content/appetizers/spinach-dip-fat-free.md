## Ingredients
- 1 pkg fat free Original Ranch Mix
- 1 pint fat free Sour Cream
- 10 oz Frozen Spinach, thaw & drain.
- 8 oz can Water Chestnuts, chopped
- 1 small round Pumpernickel Bread

## Instructions
Mix all ingredients together.

Make a cavity in center of bread & fill with dip mixture. Save & cube bread from cavity for dipping.
