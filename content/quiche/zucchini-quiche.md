## Ingredients
- 3 Zucchini, medium
- 1/2 cup Onion, chopped
- 1 cup Bisquick Mix
- 4 Eggs, beaten
- 1/2 cup Oil
- 1/4 tsp Garlic Powder
- 1/2 tsp Parsley
- 1/4 tsp Oregano
- 1/2 cup Parmesan Cheese

## Instructions
Combine all ingredients. Place in greased 10" pie dish. Bake @ 350F for 40 min.
