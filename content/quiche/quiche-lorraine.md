## Ingredients
- 1 Pie Crust for 9" Pie
- 1/2# Bacon, fried & crumbled
- 1 cup Swiss Cheese, shredded
- 1/3 cup Onion, minced
- 4 Eggs
- 2 cups Light Cream
- 3/4 tsp Salt
- 1/4 tsp Sugar
- 1/8 tsp Cayenne Pepper

## Instructions
Heat oven 425F. Line prepared crust into 9" pie dish. Sprinkle with bacon, cheese & onion. With electric beater, blend remaining ingredients. Pour into crust. Bake 15 min. Reduce temp. to 300F. Bake 40 min longer or until knife inserted 1 " from edge comes out clean. Let pie stand 10-15 min before cutting.
