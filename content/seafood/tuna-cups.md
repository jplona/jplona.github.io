## Ingredients
- 1 small can Tuna
- 1/2 Cup green Celery tops, chopped
- 16 Soda Crackers
- 1 tsp Parsley or Sweet Basil
- 1 Egg
- 1 small Onion, diced
- Grated Cheese, Pepper & Salt

## Instructions
Mix ingredients in bowl & mold into 1/4 measuring cup. Remove from cup & place in muffin cups. Cover with foil & bake 30 min at 350F.

Yield: 6 tuna cups, serving 3
