Title: Grilled Salmon

Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 1 hr
Servings: 8 - 12

## Ingredients
- 1 (5 to 8 lb) Whole Salmon
- 2 tbsp Butter or Margarine
- 1/2 medium Onion, sliced
- 1/2 Lemon, sliced
- 2 or 3 sprigs fresh Parsley
- Oil & Lemon Wedges
- Salt & Pepper

## Instructions
Wash Salmon & pat dry. Sprinkle inside with salt & pepper, dot with butter. Arrange overlapping slices of onion, lemon & parsley in cavity. Brush salmon with oil. Wrap in heavy-duty alum. foil. Seal edges with double fold. Place on grill. Carefully turn salmon every 10 min. Grill for 45 min. Test for doneness. Serve with lemon wedges.
