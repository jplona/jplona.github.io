## Ingredients
- 1 # Fresh Shrimp
- 1 Clove Garlic
- 1/8 tsp Salt
- 1/8 cup Soy Sauce
- 1/8 cup Lemon Juice
- 1 tbsp chopped Parsley
- 3/4 tsp dehydrated Onion
- 1/8 tsp Pepper

## Instructions
Split shrimp & devein, leaving tails & shell on. Arrange shrimp in a shallow dish. Mash garlic with salt in small bowl, stir in remaining ingredients. Pour marinade over shrimp. Turn & let soak for 15 minutes on each side. Grill or broil for 3 to 5 minutes basting several times. Use remaining marinade as extra sauce. Yield: 2 servings.
