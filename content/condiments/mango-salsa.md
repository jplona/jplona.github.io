## Ingredients
- 1 Mango, ripe, pitted, peeled, diced
- 1/4 red or sweet Onion, diced
- 1 Jalapeno Pepper, minced
- 2 tbsp Lime Juice
- 2 tbsp Cilantro, minced
- 1/4 tsp Cumin, ground
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
Combine ingredients in a nonreactive bowl and chill one hour. Serve with grilled fish, poultry or meat.
