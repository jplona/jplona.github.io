## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup Pickling Spice
- 1 Qt Cider Vinegar
- 2 cups Water
- 2 cups Sugar
- 15 oz Tomato Sauce
- 14 oz bottle Catsup
- 12 oz can Tomato Paste
- 1/2 cup Brown Sugar, packed
- 1/2 cup Butter or Margarine
- 1/4 cup Lemon Juice
- 1 Large Onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire Sauce
- 1 tbsp Salt
- 1 tbsp Chili Powder
- 1 tbsp Garlic Powder
- 1 tbsp Crushed Red Pepper Flakes
- 1/4 tsp Hot Pepper Sauce

## Instructions
Measure pickling spice into small square of cheesecloth, gather cheesecloth around spice & tie ends together with string. Place in large kettle; add remaining ingredients & simmer 1 1/2 hrs. Remove cheesecloth. Store sauce, tightly covered, in refrigerator.
