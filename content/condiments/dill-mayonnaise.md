## Ingredients
- 1 lg Egg, room temp.
- 1 Yolk from a hard-cooked Egg
- 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
- 2 tbsp Lemon Juice
- 1 cup Olive or Canola Oil
- Salt & Pepper to taste
- 1/2 cup Dill, finely chopped

## Instructions
Combine egg, yolk, mustard & lemon juice in a food processor or blender. With motor running, add oil in slow steady stream through the feed tube until emulsified. Add salt and pepper to taste. Stir in dill by hand. Store in refrigerator up to five days.
