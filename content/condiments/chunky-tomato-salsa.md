## Ingredients
- 1 cup Grape Tomatoes, quartered
- 1 tbsp Red Onion, finely minced
- 1 Jalapeno Chile, seed & finely mince
- 1/2 tsp Salt

## Instructions
In small bowl, combine all ingredients. Makes 4 servings as garnish.
