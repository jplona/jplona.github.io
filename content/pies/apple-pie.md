## Ingredients
- 6 Cups sliced apples (Use Jonathan or Granny)
- 3/4 tsp. Cinnamon
- 3 tbsp. Tapioca
- 1 Cup Sugar
- Butter

## Instructions
Toss apples with dry ingredients. Place into unbaked pie crust. Dot with butter. Cover with top crust. Bake @ 400 F for 15 min. Then at 350 F for 30 min.
