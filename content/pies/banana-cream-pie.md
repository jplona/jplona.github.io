## Ingredients
- 9" baked Pie Crust
- 3 tbsp Cornstarch
- 1 2/3 cups Water
- 14 oz Sweetened Condensed Milk
- 3 Egg Yolks, beaten
- 2 tbsp Butter or Margarine
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- 3 medium Bananas
- ReaLemon Juice Concentrate
- Whipped Cream

## Instructions
In heavy saucepan, dissolve cornstarch in water; stir in sweetened condensed milk & egg yolks. Cook & stir until thickened & bubbly. Remove from heat; add margarine & vanilla. Cool slightly. Slice 2 bananas; dip in ReaLemon & drain. Arrange on bottom of prepared crust. Pour filling over bananas; cover. Chill 4 hrs or until set. Spread top with whipped cream. Slice remaining banana; dip in ReaLemon & garnish top of pie.
