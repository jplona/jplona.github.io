## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp Cornstarch
- 6 tbsp White Sugar
- 1/2 cup Water
- 3 Egg Whites
- Few grains Salt

## Instructions
Mix cornstarch, sugar, water & salt & cook thick & clear. Cool slightly. Beat egg whites until frothy. Continue beating while slowly pouring cooked mixture over, and continue beating 5 minutes. Cover cooled filling in pie shell with meringue sealing edges to crust. Bake at 450 F for 5 to 7 min until golden.
