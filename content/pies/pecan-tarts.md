## Ingredients
- 2 (3 oz) pkgs Cream Cheese
- 1/2 cup Light Brown Sugar, packed
- 3/4 cup Butter or Margarine
- 1 1/2 cup All Purpose Flour, heaping
- 1/2 tsp Vanilla
- 1/2# Pecans, chopped
- 2 Eggs, slightly beaten
- 3 tbsp Butter, melted

## Instructions
Mix cream cheese, butter & flour until thoroughly blended. Press dough onto bottom & up sides of ungreased muffin cups, fitting in carefully to make sure no air bubbles form.

FILLING: Add brown sugar, vanilla, pecans & melted butter to slightly beaten eggs & mix. Drop 1 tsp of filling into each shell. Bake in 325F oven about 30 min. Remove tarts from pans as soon as baked. Yield: 48 small tarts. If mixture is too thick, add 2 tbsp corn syrup or honey.
