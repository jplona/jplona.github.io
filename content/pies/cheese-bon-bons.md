## Ingredients
- 2 - 8 oz pkgs Cream Cheese
- 3/4 cup Sugar
- 2 Eggs
- 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- Crushed Vanilla Wafers

## Instructions
Place 1 tsp crushed wafers in bon bon cups. With electric mixer beat remaining ingredients until smooth. Fill cups with mixture almost to top. Bake 350F for 20 min.

Optional: Place cherry pie filling on each bon bon.
