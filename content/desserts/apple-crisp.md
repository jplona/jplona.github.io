Title: Apple Crisp
Categories: Desserts
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 20 min
Servings: 6 - 8
Source: 
Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 6 cups sliced peeled Apples
- 1/4 cup Sugar or Honey
- 1 tsp Cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp Salt
- 2 tbsp Butter or Margarine, melted
- 3 tbsp Butter or Margarine
- 1/3 cup Sugar
- 1 tbsp Flour
- 1 1/2 cups Post Raisin Bran

## Instructions
Mix apples with 1/4 cup sugar, cinnamon & salt. Stir in melted butter. Spoon into 1 1/2 qt shallow baking dish. Cream butter, blend in 1/3 cup sugar & the flour. Stir in cereal; sprinkle over apple mixture. Cover; bake at 350 F for 30 min. Uncover; bake 10 to 15 min longer, until apples are tender. Serve warm with whipped topping, if desired.
