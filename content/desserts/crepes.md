## Ingredients
- 1 cup All-Purpose Flour
- 1/4 tsp Salt
- 1 1/4 cups Milk
- 2 Eggs
- 2 tbsp Butter or Margarine, melted
- Vegetable Oil

## Instructions
Combine flour, salt, & milk, beating at medium speed of electric mixer until smooth. Add eggs & beat well; stir in melted butter. Refrigerate batter 1 hr. Brush bottom of 6" crepe pan with oil. Heat pan until hot. Pour 2 tbsp batter into pan, tilting pan in all directions to spread batter. Cook 1 min. Flip crepe & cook other side 30 seconds. Place crepes on towel & allow to cool.

## Notes
Filling: Use Preserves, cottage cheese, etc. Cherries, etc
