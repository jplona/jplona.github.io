## Ingredients
- 1 1/4 cups Boiling Water
- 1 pkg ( 8 serving size) or 2 pkg ( 4 serving size) any flavor

## Instructions
Lightly wipe inside of mold with vegetable oil or spray mold lightly with no-stick cooking spray. Stir boiling water into gelatin in medium bowl at least 3 min. until dissolved. Pour into mold to within 1/8 " of top. Refrigerate at least 3 hrs or until firm. Dip bottom of mold in warm water about 15 seconds. Pull gelatin away from all edges of mold with index finger. Slip finger underneath JIGGLERS & gently lift from mold.
