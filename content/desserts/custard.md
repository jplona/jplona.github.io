Servings: 4

## Ingredients
- 4 Egg Yolks
- 1 cup Sugar
- 2 tbsp Butter
- 1 1/2 tsp Vanilla
- 1 cup Milk

## Instructions
Beat egg yolks & sugar until ribbons form. Add 1 cup hot milk. Cook until thick. Add 2 tbsp chilled butter. Add Vanilla.

Top with fresh fruit----raspberries, peaches, blueberries or strawberries
