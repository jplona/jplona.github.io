## Ingredients
- 2 cups Flour
- 1/4 tsp Salt
- 2/3 cup Crisco
- 5 tbsp Water
### Filling
- 1/2 cup sugar, 1/4 cup flour, 1/4 tsp Cinnamon, 6 Granny Smith Apples, Butter
### Syrup
- 1 Cup Sugar, 2 cups Water
- 4 tbsp Butter
- 1 tsp Cinnamon

## Instructions
Mix flour & salt. Cut in Crisco. Add water & shape into ball. Roll into 12 x 18" rectangle. Cut into 6 equal squares. Mix sugar, flour & cinnamon. Place 1 apple, 2 tbsp sugar mixture & small piece of butter on each dough square. Bring corners of dough up around apple & pinch together edges. Place in 8 x 12" pan. Combine syrup ingredients & boil until sugar dissolves. Cover dumplings with syrup & bake at 350 F for 1 hour.
