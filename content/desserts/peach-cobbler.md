Title: Peach Cobbler
Categories: Desserts
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 20 min
Servings: 6

Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 3 cups Peaches
- 2/3 cup Sugar
- 2 tbsp Flour
- 1/4 tsp Cinnamon
### Dough Mixture
- 1 cup Flour
- 1 tsp Salt
- 1 1/2 tsp Baking Powder
- 1 Egg
- 3 tbsp Milk
- 2 tbsp Sugar
- 1/3 cup Crisco

## Instructions
Arrange peaches in pie dish. Sprinkle with mixture of sugar, flour & cinnamon. For dough mixture: Combine dry ingredients. Add egg, milk & crisco. Drop dough by tablespoon onto peaches. Bake 350F for 25-30 min.
