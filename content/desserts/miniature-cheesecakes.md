## Ingredients
- 24 cupcake liners ( 2 1/2")
- 24 Vanilla Wafers
- 16 oz. Cream Cheese, softened
- 3/4 cup Sugar
- 2 Eggs
- 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- 20 oz can Cherry or Blueberry Filling
- 1/2 cup sliced Nuts, optional

## Instructions
Line cupcake pans with liners. Place a vanilla wafer in bottom of each liner. In small bowl beat cream cheese, sugar, eggs, lemon juice & vanilla until light & fluffy. Fill the liners 3/4 full with cheese mixture. Bake in preheated oven for 15 to 20 min. or until set. Top each with spoonful of filling & 1 tsp of nuts. Chill.

## Notes
Option: Use 48 (1 3/4") liners for Bon Bons.
