## Ingredients
- 3 lg or 4 sml firm but ripe Bananas
- 2 1/2 tbsp Butter
- 2 1/2 tbsp Brown Sugar
- 1/4 tsp grated Nutmeg
- 1/2 cup Vanilla Yogurt

## Instructions
Peel bananas & cut into long diagonal slices about 1/2" thick. In large skillet, cook the butter & brown sugar over medium heat, stirring, until sugar dissolves. Place bananas in syrup in a single layer, reduce the heat to med-low & cook 3 to 4 min. until browned. Turn with a spatula & cook on the second side until bananas are soft & richly browned. Top with yogurt & nutmeg. Serve warm.
