Title: Cherry Squares
Categories: Desserts
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 30 min
Servings: 12-14

Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 2 sticks Margarine or Butter
- 1 cup Sugar
- 2 Eggs, beat in small bowl
- 2 cups Flour
- 1/2 tsp Baking Powder
- 1/2 cup Walnuts, chopped
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- 1 (21 oz can) Cherry Pie Filling

## Instructions
Cream margarine & sugar. Add beaten eggs. Add dry ingredients & vanilla. Mix & spread 3/4 of batter in greased 9 x 13 pan. Cover with cherry filling, sprinkle with walnuts. Spread remaining batter on top as for cobbler. Bake 350F for 45 min.
