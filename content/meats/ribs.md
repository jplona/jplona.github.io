## Ingredients
- 6 to 8# Spare Ribs
- 2 tsp Cornstarch
- 2 tbsp Soy Sauce
- 8 1/2 oz Can Crushed Pineapple
- 3 tbsp Honey
- 2 tbsp Cider Vinegar

## Instructions
Combine pineapple & cornstarch. Add honey, soy sauce, & vinegar. Heat 5 min. Steam ribs in heavy double thickness alumifoil with 2 tbsp water. Cook on grill for 1 hr. Do not open packet while steaming. Remove ribs & place over heat generously basting with sauce. Cook another 30 min, 15 on each side, being careful not to burn sauce.
