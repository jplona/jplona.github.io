## Ingredients
- 8 oz Cream Cheese, softened
- 1/2 stick Butter or Margarine, softened
- 4 cups Conf. Sugar
- 2 tsp Vanilla
- Milk, as needed

## Instructions
In mixer bowl, combine cream cheese & butter, beat till smooth. Using lowest speed, gradually add conf. sugar & beat. Add vanilla. If needed, add a little milk to thin frosting. Frosts one cake.
