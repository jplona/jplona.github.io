## Ingredients
- 3 oz Cream Cheese, softened
- 1 tbsp Butter, softened
- 1 tsp Orange Juice
- 1 tsp Grated Orange Peel
- 1 1/2 cups Conf. Sugar, sifted

## Instructions
In small mixer bowl, beat cream cheese, butter, orange juice & orange peel until light & fluffy. Gradually add sugar, mixing until fluffy & of spreading consistency. If it is too thin, add a little more sugar. Makes enough for a 13 x 9" cake.
