## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp Butter or Margarine
- 4 tsp Flour
- 1 1/2 cup Half-and Half
- 1/4 cup Parmesan Cheese, grated
- 1 pkg Louis Kemp Crab Delights
- 6 oz Fettucini, cooked
- 1 tbsp Parsley, fresh & chopped

## Instructions
Melt butter, stir in flour. Gradually add half and half, stir until thickened. Add Parmesan, cook and stir until smooth. Fold in Crab Delights, heat 3 min, stir occasionally. Toss fettucini with sauce, sprinkle with parsley. Serve immediately.
