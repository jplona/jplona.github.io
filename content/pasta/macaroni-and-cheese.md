## Ingredients
- 2 tsps Onion, grated
- 1/2 # Cheddar Cheese, grated
- 1 1/2 cups Milk, scalded
- 1/2 tsp Salt
- 1/4 tsp black or white Pepper
- 2 cups Elbow Macaroni, uncooked
- 1/2 cup bread crumbs, tossed in 3 tbsp melted butter
- 1 tbsp Paprika

## Instructions
Preheat oven 375F. Over med. heat, add onion & half grated cheese to scalded milk. Stir constantly until cheese melts. Add salt and pepper. Cook macaroni 5 to 10 min. Drain & rinse. Alternate layers of macaroni & cheese sauce in a buttered 1 1/2 qt. casserole. Sprinkle with remaining grated cheese, bread crumbs & paprika. Bake uncovered 20-25 min until crumbs are browned.

Note: To scald milk, heat it slowly in a small pan until tiny bubbles appear around the edge, but before the milk boils up.
