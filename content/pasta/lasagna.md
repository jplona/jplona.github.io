## Ingredients
- 2 1/4 cup Spaghetti Sauce, (28 oz)
- 1# Ground Beef, cooked & drained
- 2 cups Cottage Cheese
- 1 Egg, beaten
- 6 Lasagna Noodles, cooked, drained & rinsed
- 2 cups Kraft Mozzarella Cheese, Shredded Low-Moisture Part-Skim (divided)

## Instructions
Mix sauce & cooked meat. Set aside. Mix cottage cheese & egg until blended. Spoon 1 c of sauce mixture on bottom of 12x8 baking dish. Layer half of lasagna noodles, half of cottage cheese mixture, one-third of cheese & half of remaining sauce mixture in dish. Repeat layers, ending with sauce. Spread with remaining shredded cheese. Bake 350F for 45 min. Let stand 10 min before serving.
