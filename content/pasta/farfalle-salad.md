## Ingredients
- 12 oz Farfalle
- 3 tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- 1/2 cup Fresh Basil Leaves, loosely packed
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp Salt
- 1/4 cup Sugar
- 1/3 cup Parmesan Cheese, grated
- 1/2 cup Olive Oil
- Black Pepper, to taste
- Cherry Tomatoes for garnish

## Instructions
Cook pasta according to package directions. Drain & cool. In blender combine vinegar, basil, garlic, salt, sugar, cheese & olive oil. Blend until smooth. Combine with pasta. Cover & refrigerate to blend flavors. Sprinkle with pepper & garnish with cherry tomatoes before serving.
