## Ingredients
- 8 oz Sea-Shell Macaroni (about 3 1/2 c), cooked & drained
- 1 1/4 cups prepared Salsa
- 16 oz can Black Beans, rinse & drain
- 1 pkg (10 oz) frozen corn, thawed

## Instructions
In large bowl combine sea-shells, salsa, beans & corn. Serve at room temperature.

NOTE: to serve hot, combine salsa, beans & corn in large skillet; heat through. Toss with hot sea-shells.
