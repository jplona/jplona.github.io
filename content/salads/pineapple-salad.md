Title: Pineapple Salad
Categories: Salads
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 20 min
Servings: 8
Source: 
Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 1 lg can crushed Pineapple
- 1 box Strawberry gelatin
- 1 cup Cottage Cheese (1#)
- 1 lg Cool whip
- 1 cup Pecans

## Instructions
Heat pineapple & stir in gelatin until dissolved, cool thoroughly. Fold in cottage cheese, pecans & cool whip. Chill until firm & serve.
