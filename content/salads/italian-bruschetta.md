## Ingredients
- 1 pint Grape Tomatoes, halved
- 2 Celery Ribs, diced
- 1/4 cup Black Pitted Olives, sliced
- 1/3 cup Stuffed Green Olives, sliced
- 1/4 cup minced Parsley
- 1 sml clove Garlic, peeled & minced
- 1 lg Carrot, julienne
- 1/3 sweet Onion, finely minced
- 1 tbsp Virgin Olive Oil
- 1/2 tbsp Balsamic Vinegar
- Salt & freshly ground pepper to taste

## Instructions
Combine all ingredients in a large bowl. Taste & correct seasoning , if needed. Let stand at room temperature for 10 min. Serve over grilled sliced Italian or French bread. Slice bread about 3/4" thick.
