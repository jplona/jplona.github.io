Title: Seven Layer Salad
Categories: Salads
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 30 min
Servings: 10

Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 1 head Lettuce, shredded (1/2 Bowl)
- 1/2 cup Green Pepper, chopped
- 1/2 cup Celery, chopped
- 1/2 cup Scallions, chopped
- 1 box frozen peas, cook & drain
- 1 1/2 cups Hellman's Salad Dressing
- 1 tbsp Sugar
- 8 slices Bacon, cook & crumble
- 1 cup Cheese, shredded (use Swiss or Cheddar)

## Instructions
In large bowl layer lettuce, green pepper, celery, scallions & peas. Spread salad dressing over top. Sprinkle with sugar & bacon. Add cheese.

Cover with saran wrap. Refrigerate for at least 24 hours. Toss when ready to serve.
