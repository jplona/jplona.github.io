## Ingredients
- 20 oz Crushed Pineapple, undrained
- 8 oz Cream Cheese, softened
- 1 lg pkg Instant Vanilla Pudding Mix
- 12 oz Pineapple chunks, drained
- 8 oz Cool Whip, thawed

## Instructions
Gradually stir undrained pineapple into softened cream cheese. Stir in pudding mix. When it starts to thicken fold in pineapple chunks & whipped topping. Pour into 9 x 13" dish. Chill.

Pie Filling: Gently fold 1 to 2 sml bananas, sliced into mixture. Spoon into two 8" ready to use graham cracker pie crusts. Smooth mixture. Garnish with blueberries or strawberries. Chill overnight.
