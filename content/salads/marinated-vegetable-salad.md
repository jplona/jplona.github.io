## Ingredients
- 1 cup Broccoli Florets, blanched
- 1 cup Cauliflower Florets, blanched
- 1 cup Celery, sliced
- 1 cup Carrots, sliced
- 1/2 cup Green Pepper, chopped
- 1/2 cup Red Onion, thinly sliced
- 4 oz Kidney Beans, cook & drain
- 4 oz Garbanzo Beans, cook & drain
- 10 sml Black Olives, sliced
- 3/4 cup Italian Dressing, reduced-calorie

## Instructions
Combine all ingredients in large bowl, tossing well. Refrigerate at least 3 hours or overnight.
