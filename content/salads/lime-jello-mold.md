Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 1 cup Boiling Water
- 1 Lime Jello, lg pinch of Salt
- 1 #2 can Crushed Pineapple, undrained
- 1 pt Sour Cream

## Instructions
Combine Boiling Water & large package Lime Jello. Add Salt. Add pineapple & Sour Cream. Pour into lightly oiled mold. Refrigerate over night.
