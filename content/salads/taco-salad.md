## Ingredients
- 1 Head Lettuce, shred as for coleslaw
- 1 # Grd Chuck, brown & crumble
- 1 med Onion, chopped
- 1 Green Pepper, chopped
- 1 # Sharp Cheddar Cheese, shredded
- 2 Tomatoes, cubed
- 1 10 oz bag Taco Chips, crushed
- 4 Scallions, chopped
- 1 - 8oz Catalina Dressing

## Instructions
Brown meat & onion. Drain off excess fat. Add remaining ingredients. Toss with Catalina dressing.

Optional - Add one bottle of Mild Salsa or Mild Sauce (Ortega) to browned meat if desired. Use more meat if desired.
