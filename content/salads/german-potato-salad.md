Title: German Potato Salad
Categories: Salads, Vegetables
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 20 min
Servings: 6
Source: 
Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 12 Potatoes
- 1/2 cup Water
- 1/2 medium Onion
- 1 tsp Flour
- 4 slices Bacon
- 1 hard boiled Egg
- 1-1/2 cup Vinegar
- 1/2 cup Sugar
- Parsley, chopped
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
Cook potatoes until almost done but still firm. Slice warm potatoes , adding chopped onion & salt. Set aside in warm place. Cut up bacon & fry until crisp. Remove from heat & add a mixture of vinegar, sugar, water & flour. Return to heat & cook until it thickens. Add to potatoes. Sprinkle with chopped eggs & parsley . Serve warm.
