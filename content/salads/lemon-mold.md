## Ingredients
- 1 lg Lemon Jello
- 2 1/2 cups Boiling Water
- 1/4 cup White Sugar
- 6 oz Lemonade Concentrate (6 oz)
- 9 oz Cool Whip

## Instructions
Mix jello , boiling water & sugar. Add 6 oz. lemonade concentrate. Refrigerate 2 hours. Remove from refrigerator & whip. Add 9 oz. cool whip. Whip & place in mold. Refrigerate over night.
