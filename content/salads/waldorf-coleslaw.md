Title: Waldorf Coleslaw
Categories: Salads
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 20 min
Servings: 6 - 8
Source: 
Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 6 cups Cabbage, shredded
- 2 cups Apples, chopped, Red & Golden Delicious
- 1/2 cup Raisins
- 1/4 cup salted Peanuts
- Miracle Whip salad dressing

## Instructions
Blend ingredients. Refrigerate.
