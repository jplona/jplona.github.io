## Ingredients
- 1 cup Almonds, chopped
- 1 1/2 cup Butter
- 1 cup Sugar
- 1 Egg
- 3 cups Cake Flour
- 1/4 tsp Salt

## Instructions
Cream butter thoroughly. Add sugar, gradually creaming it in well. Add beaten eggs, nuts & flour. Bake at 350 F for 20 minutes.
