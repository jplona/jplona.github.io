Title: Almond Crescents
Categories: Cookies
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 30 min
Servings: 4 doz

Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 1 # Margarine
- 1/2 # Conf. Sugar
- 1/2 # Almonds, chopped
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- 2 Eggs
- 4 cups Flour

## Instructions
Mix all ingredients together. Roll out small ball of dough shaping into crescent. Bake at 350F on ungreased cookie sheet for 10-12 min or until bottoms are golden brown. Roll in conf. sugar.

Yield: 4 doz.
