## Ingredients
- 2 1/2 cups Flour
- 1/2 # Butter
- 1 pkg Dry Yeast
- 3 Egg Yolks
- 1/2 pint Sour Cream
- 1/2 cup Sugar
- 1/2 cup ground Walnuts

## Instructions
Cut in flour & butter as for pie crust. Sprinkle yeast over top. Add well beaten egg yolks & sour cream. Place in refrigerator overnite. Make into small balls, pressing flat in palm of hand to size of silver dollar. Coat with sugar nut mixture & roll up into a horn. Bake on greased cookie sheet at 350 F for 25 - 30 min.
