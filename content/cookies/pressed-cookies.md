## Ingredients
- 1# Butter or Margarine
- 1 1/3 cup Sugar
- 2 Eggs, slightly beaten
- 2 tsp Vanilla
- 5 cups Flour
- 1/4 tsp Salt

## Instructions
Cream butter & sugar. Add eggs and vanilla. Mix in dry ingredients with spoon, then mix well with hands. Force dough through cookie press onto an ungreased cookie sheet. Decorate with colored sugars or candies. Bake at 400 F for 6 to 8 min.
