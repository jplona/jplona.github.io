Title: Pecan Puffs
Categories: Cookies
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 30 min
Servings: 2 doz
Source: 
Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 1/4# Margarine
- 2 tbsp Sugar
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- 1 cup Flour
- Pinch Salt
- 3/4 cup Ground Pecans

## Instructions
Cream margarine & sugar. Add flour, salt, vanilla, & pecans. Form into small balls on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake at 350F for 13-15 min or until golden brown on bottom. Cool & roll in conf. sugar.

Yield: 2 doz cookies
