## Ingredients
- 3 Eggs
- 1 cup Sugar
- 1 3/4 cup Flour
- 1/3 tsp Anise Oil

## Instructions
With electric mixer, combine unbeaten eggs & sugar. Beat for 15 min. until satin smooth. Use whisk to fold in flour, a little at a time. Drop 1/2 tsp of mixture per cookie onto cookie sheet. Let stand overnight, uncovered in warm room. Bake 350F until straw color. Because of strong aroma, store cookies separately.
