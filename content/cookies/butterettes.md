## Ingredients
- 2 1/2 sticks Butter, softened
- 2/3 cup Conf. Sugar
- 1 Egg Yolk
- 2 c Flour
- 1/2 tsp Almond Extract

## Instructions
Mix all ingredients together. Put thru press. Bake on ungreased cookie sheet @ 350F 10-12 min or until bottoms are golden brown.

Yield: 4 dozen
