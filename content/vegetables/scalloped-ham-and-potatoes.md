## Ingredients
- 4 cups heavy Whipping Cream
- 2 cloves Garlic, slivered
- 1 tsp dried Thyme leaves
- 1 tsp Salt
- 1/2 tsp Pepper
- 1 # Ham, sliced
- 2 # Potatoes, peeled & thinly sliced
- 1 lg Onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp fresh Parsley, chopped

## Instructions
Combine cream, garlic, thyme, salt & pepper in heavy saucepan. Bring to boil, reduce to simmer & cook 10 to 12 min until sauce has reduced by third. Butter shallow 8 x 14 pyrex dish. Layer 1/2 of ham, 1/2 of potatoes, 1/2 of onion. Pour 1/2 of cream mixture over layers. Repeat layering. Bake 350F for 1 hr until brown & bubbly.
