Title: Red Cabbage
Categories: Vegetables
Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 20 min
Servings: 4 - 6
Source: 
Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup Water
- 1/2 tsp Salt
- 1 500 mg Vitamin C, pulverized
- 1/3 cup Cider Vinegar
- 1/3 cup Brown Sugar
- 3 - 4 Whole Cloves
- 4 - 6 cups finely shredded Cabbage (about 1/2 large head)

## Instructions
Mix all ingredients except cabbage in saucepan & simmer 1 minute. Add cabbage & simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally until cabbage pieces are tender & a uniform raspberry color.
