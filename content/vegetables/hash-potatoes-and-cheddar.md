## Ingredients
- 2# pkg frozen hash brown potatoes
- 1 can Cream of Chicken Soup
- 1 sml Onion
- 2 cups Shredded Cheddar Cheese
- 1 pt Sour Cream

## Instructions
Thaw potatoes. Mix in remaining ingredients. In greased casserole bake at 350F for approximately 1 to 1 1/4 hrs.
