## Ingredients
- 3 cups Cabbage
- 1 cup Celery
- 1 cup Green Pepper
- 1/4 cup Onion, chopped
- 1 1/2 tsp Salt
- 2 tbsp Bacon Drippings
- 2 tbsp Water
- Pepper to Taste

## Instructions
Combine ingredients and cook about 10 min. Good with Ham or Pork.
