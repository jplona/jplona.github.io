## Ingredients
- 3 # little Red Potatoes
- 1/3 cup extra-virgin Olive Oil
- 2 tbsp prepared grainy Mustard
- 1 tsp Cayenne Pepper
- 1/2 tsp Salt

## Instructions
Heat oven 375F. Halve or quarter potatoes. Place in roasting pan. In small bowl combine oil, mustard, cayenne pepper & salt. Toss potatoes with 2/3 of the mixture. Bake uncovered 30 - 40 min, or until tender & browned. Toss potatoes with remaining mixture & warm thoroughly in oven.
