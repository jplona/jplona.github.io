Author: Mary C. Plona
Prep Time: 30 min
Servings: 6 - 8

Missing: false

## Ingredients
- 1 lg Onion
- 1 stick Margarine
- 1 can Cream of Chicken Soup
- 1/2 cup Evaporated Milk
- 8 oz jar Cheese Whiz
- 2 boxes Broccoli, chopped

## Instructions
Saute onion in margarine. Add cream of chicken soup, milk, cheese whiz & broccoli. Let mixture cook for 10 min. Cook rice in 2 cups water until tender & add to mixture. Pour mixture into greased casserole & bake at 300F for 30 min.
