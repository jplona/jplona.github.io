## Ingredients
- 3 cups unsifted Flour
- 2 cups Sugar
- 1 cup Hellmann's Mayonnaise
- 1/3 cup Milk
- 2 Eggs
- 2 tsp Baking Soda
- 1 1/2 tsp Ground Cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp Ground Nutmeg 1/2 tsp Salt
- 1/4 tsp Ground Cloves
- 1/2 cup Walnuts, coarsely chopped
- 3 cups Apples, Peeled & chopped
- 1 cup Seedless Raisins

## Instructions
Grease & flour two 9" round baking pans. In large bowl using mixer at low speed, beat first 10 ingredients 2 min scraping bowl frequently. Stir in apples, raisins & nuts. Pour into pans. Bake 45 min at 350 F or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Frost with whipped cream or frosting.
