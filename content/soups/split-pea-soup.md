## Ingredients
- 16 oz Pkg Green Split Peas
- 3 qts Water
- 1 sml Ham Shank or Hock ( 1 1/2#)
- 1 lg Onion, finely chopped
- 2 Chicken Bouillon Cubes
- 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
- 1/2 tsp Oregano Leaves
- 1/2 tsp Pepper
- 1 Bay Leaf
- 1 1/2 cup Carrots, thinly sliced
- 1 cup Celery, chopped

## Instructions
In deep pot combine peas, water, ham shank, onion, chicken bouillon & seasonings. Simmer uncovered 1 1/2 hrs. Remove ham shank, trim meat off bone & return to pot. Stir in carrots & celery. Simmer uncovered 1 1/2 to 2 hrs or until soup reaches desired thickness.
