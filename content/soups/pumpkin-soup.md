## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup Butter or Margarine
- 3/4 cup Onion, finely chopped
- 3 cups Chicken Stock
- 2 cups Cooked Pumpkin (from 2 1/2# Pumpkin)
- 1 cup Half & Half
- 1 tsp Paprika
- 1 tsp Sugar
- 1/8 tsp Chilli Powder
- Salt & Pepper to taste

## Instructions
Preheat oven to 400 F. Remove seeds & halve pumpkin. Bake a 2 1/2# pumpkin with skin side up on oiled baking sheet until skin is wrinkled & flesh is soft, about 1 hr. Cool, peel off skin. Saute onion in butter until golden. Stir in all remaining ingredients except Half & Half. Simmer 15 min. Puree mixture in blender. Return to pan & add Half & Half. Do not boil. Serve with dollop of sour cream.
